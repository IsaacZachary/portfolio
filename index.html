"Everything is possible. We just need to put in the work."

I'm a software engineer and DevOps practitioner with a BSc in Software Development from KCA University. I started in the field: from 2019 I installed CCTV and IP surveillance, configured networks and fixed whatever broke at client sites. That work taught me what production really means. It's a system somebody's business depends on at 2am.

I took that reliability mindset into software. Today I'm the founder and lead engineer of MobKazi, a business operating system for African enterprise. I design the architecture, write the code, run the CI/CD and the servers, and work with clients day to day. Products include the Business OS (POS, ERP, apps), Shauri for law firms, Sentinel/WaqForce for security patrols, SomaSteps for schools, an ISP platform and MobKazi AI.

I build with AI co-pilots and agentic workflows. That's how one engineer ships at the pace of a team. I'm a father, and my family is why I care so much about quality and building things that last.
